Sound Lab

A step sequencer that runs entirely in your browser. No signup, no upload. Tap a square to turn a sound on or off for that step, then press Play.

↔ Swipe sideways to see all 16 steps. Track names stay pinned on the left.

Tracks: Kick, Snare, Hi-hat, Clap, and a Bass synth that plays a short note pattern. Bass notes are fixed to a minor groove so it always sounds musical. Patterns A–D are saved on this device only.
